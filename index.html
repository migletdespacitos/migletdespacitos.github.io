About

I'm Miguel, a Data Science & Analytics graduate from NUS (Honours with Distinction). My work spans machine-learning pipelines, statistical modelling, and deploying Large Language Model applications, and across my projects and industry experience I care most about building clean, mathematically sound solutions.

At the Ministry of Health, I built RAG systems and automation pipelines on internal LLM APIs, implementing deliberate chunking strategies, prompt chaining, and output-verification guardrails. On the quantitative side, I architected a meta-learning routing framework that uses an XGBoost classifier to dynamically dispatch specialised deep-neural-network hedgers across simulated stochastic price paths.

I've also built end-to-end predictive models, from credit-scoring pipelines that handle class-imbalanced data via GHOST calibration to genomic sequence classifiers evaluating transcriptomic changes.

I'm interested in roles across Data Science, AI Engineering, and Quantitative Analysis, where rigorous modelling meets messy, complex data. Let's connect.

Outside of work, I enjoy chess, basketball, cycling, guitar, cooking, and following Formula 1.

Languages: English, Filipino, Mandarin (Intermediate), French (Elementary).
